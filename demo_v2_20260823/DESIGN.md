# Lavella Hair visual identity - reset 3

## Style prompt

An art-directed consultation page for a small British hair studio. It should feel like a materially rich colour notebook, not a beauty-template landing page: pale mineral paper, charcoal ink, moss green, a single acidic chartreuse signal, and one abstract hair-fibre study balanced by a real Lavella salon photograph. Reading is compact, calm and useful. The eye moves between colour material, salon evidence and the practical booking route.

## Design dials

- Variance: 6/10
- Motion: 3/10
- Density: 4/10

## Colours

- `#f4f0e8` - mineral paper
- `#19201d` - charcoal ink
- `#496050` - moss green
- `#bedb54` - booking signal
- `#d8d3c7` - soft border

## Typography

- Bricolage Grotesque - all reading and display copy
- DM Mono - concise metadata only

## Motion

One entrance sequence only: the concept image settles first, then the booking copy and button. Consultation blocks reveal on view. Duration 220-560ms; transforms and opacity only; motion is disabled for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## What not to do

- No giant headline as a substitute for composition.
- No generic AI person, salon, or before-and-after claim.
- No dark SaaS dashboard appearance, matching service cards, rating wall, decorative section numbers or scroll cues.
- No endless animation, parallax, glow effects or motion that blocks booking.
