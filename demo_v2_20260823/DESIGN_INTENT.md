# Design Intent - Lavella Hair v2 reset 3

Status: internal evidence after user-reported visual-direction failure. Date: 2026-08-23.

## Design read

Reading this as: a local salon consultation entrance for clients deciding whether to book colour, extensions or hair replacement, with a tactile colour-notebook visual language that balances one clearly conceptual material study with real salon evidence.

`DESIGN_VARIANCE 6 / MOTION 3 / DENSITY 4`.

## Merchant-specific recognition cues

- A constrained abstract colour-material study is the dedicated opening visual. It is not presented as a real client, result, person or salon.
- Lavella Hair's publicly shown green-marble wash area is a distinct real-salon evidence visual.
- The business's public logo is used once in the header.
- The exact public booking vocabulary is Colouring, Hair Extensions and Replacement Solutions, plus the women's hair replacement consultation.
- Fairclough Hall Farm, Halls Green, Weston, Hitchin, SG4 7DP is the concrete end point.

## Direction and page architecture

`abstract colour-material study / concise booking decision / real salon evidence / three practical consultation contexts / existing booking reassurance / illustrated location close`

The visual hierarchy is deliberately normal-scale, sans-serif and reading-led. The largest headline is a compact service sentence, not a fashion-cover statement. Copy explains the next decision. The opening image is an original material concept; the real salon photo substantiates the physical context in a separate section. The building sketch carries only the location close.

The page avoids oversized display type, editorial italic headlines, section numerals, caption overlays, city/scroll decoration, three equal cards, huge review figures, generic AI salon imagery, and repeated generic booking blocks.

## Closing strategy

`Reassurance + Specificity + Conversion + Brand Close`: the close adds the verified address, dated booking-profile context and existing external booking path. It does not repeat hero text or use a giant pure-colour CTA panel.

## Material and rights position

Only a minimum of public merchant material is used. `CUSTOMER AUTHORIZATION: NOT VERIFIED`. Direct sources, hashes, placement and exclusion decisions are in `IMAGE_LEDGER.md`. One constrained ImageGen concept visual is used because the available real assets did not provide a strong colour-material opening; it is never presented as a customer asset or business fact.

## Recognition test

Yes. Without its name, the real dark-green wash space, consultation-specific hair vocabulary and Fairclough Hall Farm destination still identify a particular local salon rather than a generic beauty landing page.
