# Design Intent - Lavella Hair v3

Date: 2026-08-23. Status: pre-code internal evidence after independent visual rejection of v2.

> **AUTHORITATIVE VISUAL REFINEMENT — 2026-08-23.** The earlier warm-paper revision remains historical. This addendum is the active direction for the next local rework.

## The actual job

The available public material proves a real, considered studio but does not provide a public before-and-after portfolio. The page must therefore not imitate a hair-campaign site. Its job is to make the first appointment feel legible: see a real place, recognise the three different ways to begin, then move once to the live booking system.

## Active visual grammar

- **Image first, but not image-only:** the wide branded styling floor sets place; the reception and corridor become two specific confidence moments rather than decorative interruptions.
- **A guided decision spine:** Colour, Extensions and Hair Replacement are three unequal readiness states, presented as a single vertical route with deliberate pacing instead of a generic card grid.
- **Quiet authority:** warm mineral paper, charcoal, soft bronze and very limited pale-blue detail echo the public interior. Hanken Grotesk stays compact; headings must never rely on oversize scale to create premium value.
- **One destination:** all reading leads toward one clearly labelled handoff to the existing Ovatu page. No invented contact channel, form, account, payment flow, service result, practitioner claim or availability claim.
- **Motion policy:** only opacity/transform entrances and a reduced-motion fallback may be used. Motion must reveal reading order, never add spectacle.

## Recognition test for this rework

The hero’s LAVELLA wall, the reception wordmark, the black-framed mirror runs, the specific colour/extensions/replacement distinction, and Fairclough Hall Farm must all remain necessary. Without them the page should lose its identity; it must not become a generic luxury hair template.

## Design read

Reading this as: a local hair studio's **appointment decision guide**, for a client who needs to decide whether she can choose a Colour service now or should begin with an Extensions or Hair Replacement conversation. It is neither a salon portfolio nor a beauty template.

## Recognition cues and materials

- Public Lavella Gallery material supplies a branded reception, the actual styling floor, wash area and the internal corridor. The Hero uses a recorded image-to-image horizontal continuation of the supplied styling-floor photograph so the page can show it at a legible width; it is not used as evidence of additional facilities or work results.
- The service logic comes from the current public Ovatu categories: Colouring; Hair Extensions and Replacement Solutions; and the dedicated Women's Hair Replacement System Consultation.
- Fairclough Hall Farm, Halls Green, Weston, Hitchin, SG4 7DP is the location cue. The one external conversion path is the existing Ovatu booking page.
- `CUSTOMER AUTHORIZATION: NOT VERIFIED` for customer-origin materials. Sources, hashes, placement and use limit are recorded in `IMAGE_LEDGER.md`.

## Direction

`studio observation / decision guide / reassurance / live booking handoff`

The page resembles a quiet studio lookbook with an appointment guide inside it: warm paper, forest ink, clay route markers, Hanken Grotesk, restrained titles and photo-led evidence. The hero is an image-first horizontal studio observation with a compact margin title below, not an overlay, split screen or masthead-scale headline.

The three services are not cards and do not each ask for a booking click. They are three visually distinct decision paths that converge once on the Ovatu handoff: Colour means choose in the live menu; Extensions means start with an assessment of hair, lifestyle and desired result; Hair Replacement means use the dedicated women's system consultation.

## Explicit exclusions

- No Manrope, DM Mono, black-plus-green, fluorescent yellow, split hero, centred poster headline, three equal cards, giant review total, dark close plus bright CTA, or repeating CTA per service.
- No generated human, generic salon, before-and-after, hair outcome, health claim, price, availability or review claim beyond dated public evidence.
- No treatment-room image associated with another practitioner is used.

## Closing strategy

`Specificity + Reassurance + Conversion + Brand Close`: the final route repeats neither the hero nor each service. It introduces the real studio corridor and a factual address/review context, then hands off once to the existing booking service.

## Recognition test

Yes. Without the name, the branded reception sign, black-framed styling floor, public hair-service vocabulary and Fairclough Hall Farm endpoint identify a specific local hair business rather than a transferable salon layout.
