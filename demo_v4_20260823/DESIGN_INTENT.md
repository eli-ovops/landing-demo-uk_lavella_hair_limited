# Design Intent — Lavella Hair v4

Date: 2026-08-23. Status: local concept rebuild after v3 visual-direction failure.

## First principle

Lavella’s public evidence is its real studio, its hair consultation paths, location and live Ovatu profile—not a public campaign portfolio. The page therefore earns a premium feeling by letting the visitor enter a specific space, then moving through a single editorial sequence toward the right live booking route.

## Direction

`dark arrival frame → consultation index → editorial space reassurance → live booking handoff`

- A deep charcoal opening lets the actual styling-floor photograph act like a gallery image, with brand detail anchored in the lower edge rather than copy placed on the image.
- A restrained serif is used for short statements only; compact sans-serif carries all operational detail. No oversized display text.
- Colour, Extensions and Hair Replacement are arranged as a sequential consultation index, not generic cards or a dark data table. Each route answers a genuinely different booking question before the live handoff.
- Because no verified public hair-result portfolio was suitable for display, one original non-person, non-premises colour-material study sits inside that index as a visual pause. It is internally marked as a concept asset and never presented as Lavella’s work.
- The booking area uses a restrained bordered handoff panel, so mutable facts remain visibly connected to Ovatu instead of becoming a generic oversized closing CTA.
- The reception and address are treated as a single confidence sequence; the public review total is a small factual note only.
- Motion uses only GSAP transform/opacity and one image reveal; it respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. No looping ornament or fake interaction.

## Boundaries

Only recorded public-derived Lavella interior images are used. No generated people, hair results, staff, credentials, availability, prices or new facilities are claimed. Booking, timetable, cookie preferences and reviews remain on the existing Ovatu routes.
