# Decision — RECOMMEND

## Conclusion

`RECOMMEND` — **for a narrow £600 polished static one-page Cold Demo about Lavella Hair's hair-led offer only**, subject to the existing Demo authorisation gate. It is not a recommendation to build a full multi-practitioner salon platform.

## Basis

1. **Real, current and locally controlled business:** a 2026-08-07 review sits inside the H0 window, and Companies House identifies a single active director/75%+ PSC, Olivia Lavelle. [SRC-01, SRC-05, SRC-06]
2. **A specific, reproducible presentation gap:** the current platform home routes visitors to booking/location/reviews but does not explain the hair offer; the booking surface exposes a mixed directory. A static page can supply a clear proposition before retaining Ovatu as the real CTA. [SRC-01, SRC-02]
3. **Enough truthful material for the minimum concept:** public identity, location, contact, current service structure, a bespoke-consultation description, review volume and a gallery all exist. This supports an honest hair-led narrative without inventing content. [SRC-01–SRC-04]

## Required boundary

- Narrative: **“A Weston hair destination for colour and bespoke hair-extension/replacement consultations.”**
- CTA: **Book a consultation** to the existing Ovatu booking URL.
- Do not include third-party/medical/aesthetic services or represent resident practitioners as staff.
